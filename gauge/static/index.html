doveria

“It should be.”

Around 1607, Galileo rolled a ball down a ramp and off a table. In his notes on folio 116v, some launches carry two numbers: the distance he calculated, marked doveria, and the distance he measured.

A result only counts once it could have come out wrong. Gauge freezes what run 1 found, then makes it predict a recording it has never seen.
